# RootBreath 🌿

**Free Pranayama breathing app for stress management in rural communities.**

Built by Kairav Mittal — Registered Yoga Teacher | Certified EMT
Founder, Project Root — Crewe, Virginia

## What is this?
RootBreath brings ancient Pranayama breathing science to anyone with
a smartphone — for free. Designed for rural communities like Crewe,
Virginia, where mental health resources are scarce.

## Techniques included
- Box Breathing (Sama Vritti) — stress regulation
- 4-7-8 Breathing — deep calm and sleep
- Breath of Fire (Kapalabhati) — energy and focus
- Alternate Nostril (Nadi Shodhana) — mental balance

## Why I built this
In 2024 I visited Crewe, VA and saw firsthand what stress does to
people with no access to wellness resources. I founded Project Root
to bring free yoga and breathing classes to the community. RootBreath
extends that mission digitally — making these tools available 24/7
to anyone who needs them.

Open index.html in any web browser. No installation needed.
