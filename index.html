How to practice

Sit comfortably with your spine tall. Inhale for 4 counts, hold for 4, exhale for 4, hold for 4. Used by Navy SEALs and emergency responders to regulate stress response.

Why RootBreath?

Southside Virginia is a health desert. Limited access to mental health resources, few wellness programs, and high stress from economic hardship mean communities like Crewe have almost no structured tools for stress management.

RootBreath was built to change that — bringing ancient Pranayama science to anyone with a smartphone. These techniques are taught at Project Root's weekly community yoga classes in Crewe, Virginia.

Breath costs nothing. It requires no equipment. And it works.
